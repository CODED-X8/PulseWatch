6. ARCHITECTURAL & COST TRADE-OFF ANALYSIS (EXAMINER VIVA DEFENSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Minimum Storage Duration Caveat (30-Day Rule):
   Amazon S3 requires objects to reside in the S3 Standard tier for at least 30 consecutive
   days before transitioning to Standard-Infrequent Access (S3 Standard-IA). Objects transitioned
   prior to 30 days incur prorated storage fees equal to 30 days of Standard storage.

2. Minimum Billable Object Size Caveat (128 KB Rule):
   Standard-IA and Glacier flexible classes enforce a minimum billable object size of 128 KB.
   Small incident CSV export files (e.g. ~1 KB to 5 KB) are billed at the full 128 KB capacity
   when transitioned to Standard-IA. For high volumes of tiny files, lifecycle transition can
   actually increase monthly spend compared to keeping them in Standard or expiring them directly.
   Therefore, the 30-day / 90-day / 365-day lifecycle tiering is designed for enterprise
   compliance and audit retention, while daily operational reports are purged automatically.
